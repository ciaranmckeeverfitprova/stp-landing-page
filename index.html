Where it started

Started In
New York.
Brought Home.

In the summer of 2023, Jack set up a fitness class at an outdoor running track in New York for a group of Irish-American mothers. No gym. No equipment. Just structure, community, and showing up together.

The feedback wasn't about how people looked. It was about energy, mental health, confidence, and feeling like themselves again.

Coming home to rural Ireland, the question was simple: why can't people here have the same?
